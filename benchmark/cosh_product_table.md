Components are written `multiplicity·[Dynkin labels]` with the dimension as a subscript; for long decompositions the two largest components are shown and the complete list (up to 400 components) is in `cosh_product_full.md`. Times are wall-clock seconds with 4 threads, excluding compilation. Every row satisfies dim R = 2^{#Φ⁺(r) + m₀}.
